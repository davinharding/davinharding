## Hi, I'm Davin 👋

**Senior Full-Stack AI Engineer.** I design, build and ship production AI products, from agent platforms inside enterprise software to consumer apps people pay for.

- 🤖 **By day:** I architect and lead the AI agent platform at **ApparelMagic**: domain-specific agents embedded in an apparel ERP, with tool-driven data access, queue-based job execution and human-in-the-loop approvals.
- 🧪 **Also:** Founder of **[Harding Labs](https://hardinglabs.io)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/Harding-Labs)), an independent AI product studio. Two products are live and taking payments: [StageSnap](https://stagesnap.xyz) (AI virtual staging for real estate photos) and [Vibecation](https://vibecation.xyz) (your Instagram becomes a travel profile and a day-by-day trip).
- 🛠️ **Background:** 8+ years full stack across enterprise consulting, SaaS and Web3, now focused on agentic systems.
